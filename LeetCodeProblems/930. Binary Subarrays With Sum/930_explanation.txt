-intution
 since we only care about the number of subarrays thats sum = target, not actually extracting those subarrays, we can
 just get the count of all subarrays thats sum <= target , and the count of all subarrays thats count <= target - 1
 and count1 - count2 = number of subarrays that sum = target.
*keyidea is that we are getting the number of subarrays thats sum equal to or less than target, which is much easier than
 trying to get the subarrays thats sum exactly equals target.



-how can do
 basically we keep track of a window with 2 indeces, one to the left of the window and the other is to the right.
 at each step, as long as the sum inside of the widow is not bigger than target: we increment the number of valid sub
 arrays ( sub arrays thats sum <= target ) by right - left + 1, this basically gets us how many subarrays we can make that
 ENDs at right, notice that we are saying ENDING at right
 ex : nums = [1,0,1,0,1], goal = 2, we are looking for every subarray that has a sum of 2 or less
 starting with r = 0, l =0, sum of that window = 1 ( sum += nums[r] which is one), since this is less or equal to 2
 valid_arrs += r - l + 1: valid_arrs =1, meaning ( at this window we have 1 valid subarray thats sum less or equal to target)
 next; l =0, r= 1, sum = 1 still, so we can increment valid_arrs as well: r - l +1 (valied_arrs = 1 + 2  ) = 3
 at r = 2, sum = 2, thats an error because we have to make sum always less or equal to target, so enter while loop
 and as long as sum > target we keep removing the current l from sum then moving sum to the right:
 sum -= nums[l] , l++, and we keep doing that as long as sum>target and l doesnt go out of range.
 once that loop ends, we increment valid_arrs as we did before ( since the array is binary) it is guarented that
 if at some point sum was bigger than target, after moving l an amount , the sum would be equal to target when it
 gets out of the loop since its discrete steps only.
 
 what if target = 0 and nums = 1 1 1 1 1 1 1 1
 we go into arr, l =0 , r=0, sum = 1, we go into loop: we keep removing sum[l] and incrementing l, but sum then starts
 reaching negative values, and gets further right than the right variable itself
 we solve this by simply asking, if left > right after we get out of the while loop?
 then just continue and dont increment valid_arrs, this ensures that we only do increment when right is finally in front 
 of left and by then the sum would have been turned positive again.
